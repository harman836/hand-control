Pointer

Pinch to move · quick pinch clicks · hold to drag

How it works

  1. Turn on Control (top right) to let gestures drive your desktop. Pause stops them without turning anything off.
  2. Hold a pose — the ring at the bottom fills, then the bound action fires.
  3. Pinch thumb and index to move the cursor; a quick pinch clicks, a held pinch drags.
  4. Map poses to actions in Gestures, adjust recognition in Pose tuning, and the cursor in Pointer.

Press Esc or click the camera to close a panel. Changes save automatically.
